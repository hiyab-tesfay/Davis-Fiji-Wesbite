What makes Delta Chi distinct

Established values. A chapter still shaping its story.

Our UC Davis chapter combines the foundation of a fraternity founded in 1848 with the energy of a chapter chartered here in 2018.

That gives members both structure and ownership. They join an international fraternity grounded in five enduring values while helping define the standards, relationships and traditions of Delta Chi at UC Davis.

We believe the experience should be more than a social calendar. At its best, membership asks students to manage real commitments, serve with others, communicate across differences and become dependable members of a community.

The potential value of membership

Growth through real responsibility.

No organization can guarantee an outcome. It can, however, create meaningful opportunities for students who choose to engage fully.

01

Belonging and support

A smaller community within UC Davis where students can build lasting friendships and find people who notice when they need encouragement.

02

Leadership and collaboration

Opportunities to help manage budgets, events, recruitment, service and chapter operations alongside other members.

03

Communication and judgment

Practice navigating decisions, resolving problems, following through on commitments and working with people who bring different perspectives.

04

Service and perspective

Spend time contributing to local organizations and campus initiatives alongside people who make service a shared priority.

05

Connections beyond college

Become part of a fraternity that describes brotherhood as “Not for College Days Alone,” including relationships with Graduate Brothers.

Expectations matter

Membership comes with responsibility.

The opportunities of fraternity life should be matched by clear expectations.

Members are expected to keep academics at the center of their college experience, act with integrity, use sound judgment, manage their time and remain accountable to one another, the university and the fraternity.

We encourage students and families to ask direct questions about time, finances, conduct, health and safety before making a decision. Official resources are linked below so those conversations can be informed.
