What brotherhood means here

A community you help create.

The strongest brotherhood is not built by simply sharing a name. It grows through time, trust, responsibility and the willingness to care for people beyond yourself.

At Delta Chi, we want members to have people they can celebrate with, learn from and confide in. We also believe support includes honest accountability: encouraging each other to follow through, make sound decisions and keep growing.

Through service, study, chapter responsibilities and ordinary time together, those relationships become something larger than a college experience.
