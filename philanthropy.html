Philanthropy & service

Service beyond ourselves.

Service is one of Phi Gamma Delta’s five values. At Delta Chi, we work to make it visible through fundraising, hands-on volunteering and relationships with community organizations.

Impact through action

Giving back is part of how we live our values.

Our chapter has raised more than $20,000 for charitable causes. Beyond the total, the purpose is to give members meaningful ways to serve together and support organizations doing important work in our region.

Community organizations

Partners in a stronger community.

Our chapter’s community work has included support for organizations serving Yolo County, the Sacramento region and animals in need.

Empower Yolo

Promoting safe, healthy and resilient communities through crisis support, shelter, counseling, legal assistance and local resource services.

Homeward Bound

An all-volunteer Golden Retriever rescue providing care, adoption support and sanctuary in the greater Sacramento area.

Feed Sacramento Homeless

Providing meals, clothing and essentials through monthly outreach, direct support and annual holiday programs.

Campus community

Showing up for one another.

Philanthropy also connects us to the wider UC Davis Greek community.

Our members participate in and support events led by campus sororities, helping their philanthropic efforts reach further while building stronger relationships across the community.

Event names and formats may change from year to year; follow the hosting organizations for current information.
