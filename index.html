Our chapter

A young chapter with a lasting foundation.

Phi Gamma Delta began at UC Davis on February 28, 2016 and was chartered as the Delta Chi Chapter on May 19, 2018.

Rooted in five core values, we are building a brotherhood where members support one another, grow as leaders and contribute to the Davis community. Our chapter belongs to a fraternity founded in 1848, while our own traditions at UC Davis are still being written.

That balance matters to us: a national history with real depth, and a local chapter with the energy to shape what comes next. We summarize the standard we want members to live in a simple chapter phrase: “Be a FIJI Gentleman.”

Five values.
One standard.

These are more than words on a page. They guide how we learn, lead, serve and show up for one another.

01

Friendship

Build relationships grounded in trust, loyalty and mutual support.

Service

04

Morality

Choose integrity, act with care and hold ourselves accountable.
