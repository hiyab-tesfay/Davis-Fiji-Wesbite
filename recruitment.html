Why recruitment matters

The future of a chapter starts with who joins it.

Recruitment is how we meet UC Davis students who share our values and are ready to contribute to something larger than themselves.

We look for people who care about their education, their relationships and the community around them. Just as importantly, recruitment gives you the chance to evaluate us: to ask direct questions, understand the responsibilities of membership and see how brothers treat one another.

The right decision should be informed and mutual. Our events are an opportunity to have those conversations in a relaxed setting.

A simple place to begin

Show up curious.

You do not need to know everything about fraternity life before attending an event. You only need to be ready for an honest conversation.

01 · Connect

Check the current schedule

Recruitment details can change each quarter. Instagram is the source for current dates, times and locations.

02 · Meet

Spend time with the chapter

Attend events, meet different brothers and get a clear sense of the people and culture.

03 · Ask

Understand the commitment

Ask about values, time, finances, academics, service, safety and anything else that matters to your decision.

04 · Decide

Choose with clarity

Consider whether the expectations, opportunities and relationships align with who you are and who you want to become.
